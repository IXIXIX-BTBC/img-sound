- IMG SOUND に入れる写真の権利は、それぞれの権利者にあります。このアプリを使っても、その権利はあなたに移りません。
- 使ってよいのは、**自分で撮った写真**、**権利者から許可を得た写真**、**利用条件が許す写真**(フリー素材など)だけです。
- **人が写っている写真**を使うときは、その人の許可を取ってください(肖像権・プライバシー)。
- **Project ファイル(Save Project の .zip)を人に渡す・ネットで配るとき**は注意してください。Project には、入れた写真そのもの・録った音・設定がすべて入っています。渡すことは「写真を配る」ことになります。配ってよい写真だけが入っていることを確かめてください。
- 写真の位置情報など(撮影場所の記録)は、アプリの音には使いませんが、Project の中の写真ファイルには残っていることがあります。

- iPhone の写真(HEIC)の変換も、ブラウザの中で行います。
- アクセス解析・広告・Cookie による追跡は使っていません。
- 公開ページは GitHub Pages(GitHub が提供する置き場)にあります。ページを開いたときの記録(IP アドレスなど)は、GitHub 側に残ることがあります([GitHub のプライバシーに関する説明](https://docs.github.com/ja/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement))。

- MIDI 機器をつなぐときは、ブラウザが MIDI の使用許可を求めます。鍵盤の入力を受け取るためだけに使います。

### 4. 音量について
- 急に大きな音が出ることがあります(特にテープエコーの Intensity を上げたとき)。音量は小さめから始めてください。
- ヘッドホン・イヤホンで長い時間、大きな音で聞かないでください。耳を傷めるおそれがあります。

## English

### 1. Rights in the photos you use
- The rights in the photos you add to IMG SOUND belong to their rights holders. Using this app does not transfer those rights to you.
- Only use **photos you took yourself**, **photos you have permission to use**, or **photos whose terms allow it** (free stock photos, for example).
- If a photo shows **people**, get their permission (portrait rights and privacy).
- **Be careful when you give someone a Project file (the .zip from Save Project) or share it online.** A Project contains the photos themselves, the recorded sounds and all settings, so sharing it means sharing the photos. Make sure it only contains photos you are allowed to share.
- The app does not use location data in photos for the sound, but it may still be inside the photo files in a Project.
- What you add, and how you use what you make, is your responsibility.

### 2. Rights in your works and commercial use
- The authors (IXIXIX from BTBC & HIDEYA KUMAGAI) claim no rights in the sounds, music and video you make with this app. **You may use your works commercially** (sell, stream, monetize, perform at paid shows, and so on).
- If a photo appears in or is used for a work, the rights in that photo still belong to its rights holders (see 1).
- **Videos that show the app's screen (including the logo)**, such as performance or tutorial videos, may be published and monetized freely.
- You may not sell or provide the app itself (including changed versions) for a fee. Contact us first if you want to ([COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md)).

### 3. Your data
- Your photos, recordings, settings and saved Sounds are stored **only in your browser (IndexedDB and localStorage)**. Nothing is sent to the authors or to any server.
- iPhone photos (HEIC) are also converted inside your browser.
- No analytics, ads or tracking cookies are used.
- The public page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep access logs (such as IP addresses) when you open it ([GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)).
- If you clear your browser data, or open the app in another browser or on another computer, your data will not be there. Keep important work with **Save Project**.
- When you connect a MIDI device, the browser asks for permission to use MIDI. It is only used to receive the notes you play.

### 4. Volume
- Sudden loud sounds can happen (especially with a high tape echo Intensity). Start with a low volume.
- Do not listen at high volume through headphones or earphones for a long time. It can damage your hearing.

- This app is provided "as is", without any warranty. The authors are not liable for any damage caused by using it, including lost data (see the [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
